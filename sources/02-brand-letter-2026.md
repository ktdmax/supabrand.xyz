# Room for the everyday


A letter and working amendment from Mara Leitner · 19.01.2026 · release 4.1
Signed with Product Council: Owen Park. Effective immediately; signature decision originally effective 1 September 2025.

## Why we exist
A city is not a race course. A journey is not always a problem to optimise. Sometimes it is ten minutes that belong to you: the long way past the river, a conversation outside the workshop, the ride home with nothing left to prove.

SUPABRAND exists to make useful movement feel more possible. We design for the life around the product — the place it is stored, the hands that repair it, the person who borrows it. Technology should leave room for attention. Progress can be quieter than a launch film.

**Purpose:** Give everyday movement more possibility, with less friction.
**Promise:** Thoughtful mobility, made to fit real life.
**Brand word:** **Spielraum.** It means room to choose, to move and to find your own way. It is our creative lens; it is not a new product name or a second mandatory tagline.

## SUPA, our working principles
The name was coined as a memorable sound, not formed as an acronym. These four principles were adopted in the 2025 culture programme as a way to live up to it:

- **S — Sinn.** Start with a reason that matters to somebody.
- **U — Umsicht.** Consider the people, resources and systems around the ride.
- **P — Präsenz.** Pay attention. Make the useful detail feel considered.
- **A — Alltag.** Make it work on an ordinary Tuesday.

For English workshops use **Sense. Understanding. Presence. Everyday life.** Do not re-engineer these words to manufacture an English acronym. In German we sometimes say: “Wir bauen nicht nur für den Weg. Wir denken an das Leben drumherum.” This is philosophy copy, not an approved environmental or safety claim.

## Expression and responsibility
We favour possibility over pressure: “A little more room in your day” is a useful direction, not text that every execution must contain. Be confident about an evidenced specification, modest about an ambition. Neither this letter nor a campaign can approve a new factual claim.

Current campaign releases may replace the master signature on the placements explicitly purchased for that campaign, during its flight dates. Endorsed-subbrand product pages use their own signature. Corporate pages about the group use the master signature, even when they show a subbrand. Geometry remains with Design Engineering; legal and evidence restrictions always survive an expression change.
