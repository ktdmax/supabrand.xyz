# Commercial naming / range release 26.3


Release memo PN-263 · Product Operations · 16 June 2026
Approved: Owen Park, Product Council. Active from 1 July 2026 until replaced by a signed range release. This memo governs commercial names and market release, including names appearing in campaigns.

| Node | DOCKGRID | SUPABRAND DOCKGRID N6 | Battery charging station; business customers |

DAILY is a line, not an endorsed brand. A8 and F4 are model identifiers, not ranges. The family names organise service documentation and need not be appended to every display name. Do not invent “SUPA Bike”, “PICO by VELA”, “VELA Pro” or “DOCKGRID Home”. First product mention uses the full display name; subsequent prose may omit SUPABRAND if brand ownership is clear on the same page. Technical part labels may use the SKU alone.

## July market release
City C1 DAILY, City C2 and VELA A8: Austria, Germany, Switzerland, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Czech Republic, United Kingdom, United States, South Korea.
FIELD F4: Austria, Germany, Switzerland, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Czech Republic, United Kingdom. Fleet enquiry only.
PICO G1: Austria, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, Czech Republic. No release for Switzerland, Netherlands, United Kingdom, United States or South Korea.
DOCKGRID N6: Austria, Germany, Switzerland, France, Italy, Netherlands, United Kingdom. Business enquiry only. Other regions require a future Product Council release; an export contact is not retail availability.

A commercial release is not an assertion that a product may be used on every road. Local road-use copy is reviewed separately. Never suggest UK public-road permission for PICO G1. Do not infer scooter availability from the bicycle portfolio's twelve-market presence.

## Localisation
Keep VELA, PICO, FIELD and DOCKGRID in capitals in Latin-script market copy. Korea consumer display uses **SUPABRAND 벨라 A8**; specification and service tables retain **VELA A8** in the model field. The Korean guide governs the surrounding sentence. Do not transliterate the SKU or create a Korean PICO launch by analogy. The masterbrand remains SUPABRAND in every script.

## Change note
City C1 was called SUPABRAND City One in 2022 launch decks. City One is a historical name and may remain in archived service histories for the first-generation non-current model. It is not a nickname for C1 DAILY. Sales shorthand does not amend this memo.
