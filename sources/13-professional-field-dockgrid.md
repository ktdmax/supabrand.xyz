Controlled deliverable of release board PS-0514, paired with Packaging Standard PK-21. The board owns professional carton legal-type specifications across both publications. Amendments are issued through the board’s signed production change record.

## Two businesses, a connected job
SUPABRAND FIELD F4 is the Cargo family's professional electric-assist cycle. SUPABRAND DOCKGRID N6 is the Node family's charging station for compatible fleet batteries. Both are sold through a business enquiry and installation/service planning process, not a direct consumer checkout. A joint installation does not make FIELD and DOCKGRID interchangeable names.

English product signatures: FIELD — **Ready for the work between places.** DOCKGRID — **A place to power the next move.** Use the relevant signature on English product heroes and brochure covers; optional on specifications, tenders and packaging. No translated professional product signatures are released in this packet. Local tenders may omit them. Corporate parent pages retain the masterbrand signature.

## Fleet language
Lead with supported use, serviceability and operating conditions. Address procurement and maintenance professionals as peers. In German-language B2B communication use Sie in Austria, Germany and Switzerland. Avoid lifestyle promises that turn an installation into “freedom for everyone”. Keep compatible battery types next to capacity numbers.

F4's 200 kg limit is maximum total permissible mass: vehicle, person and cargo together. It is not a 200 kg payload. The three-year commercial frame warranty is specific to F4 and its contract conditions. No consumer product inherits it from the SUPABRAND name.

## Integration stories
ORBITAL MESH may appear in FIELD/DOCKGRID B2B case studies only in the channels covered by the Alliance desk. A Node carton and an Instagram reel are not covered. A joint case study must include the approved relationship sentence and avoid suggesting universal compatibility.

## Production schedule: Germany F4 service carton
For the German FIELD F4 service carton legal panel, minimum legal body type is **6 pt**, solid Graphite on Paper; headings may be larger. This specification is approved in board PS-0514 and applies to that carton only. Consumer cartons and ordinary brochures use the Packaging Standard's relevant provisions. Safety markings remain subject to engineering production review and cannot be removed to make space for a logo.

## Installation responsibility
Every Node N6 installation story needs a configuration record identifying compatible F4-B2 packs. The 99.2% pilot result is a measured case study, not a contractual service-level agreement. Partner imagery rights and site consent must be cleared before publication; our technical commissioning sign-off does not clear photography.
