# VELA / room to take the long way


Consumer Design Studio · 26 January 2026 · 1.6 · approved by Mara Leitner and Owen Park

VELA is SUPABRAND's premium bicycle subbrand. Its current family is Allroad; the released model is SUPABRAND VELA A8. Premium means precision, consideration and material honesty. It does not mean a faster life or greater personal worth. Speak to the person who notices a quiet detail and chooses to keep it.

## Product signature
**The long way, well considered.** is the English VELA product signature. In VELA product pages, brochures and non-campaign product films it takes the signature position instead of the masterbrand line. It is optional on specification sheets and packaging. Corporate stories about the group retain the masterbrand signature. In Korea omit the English product signature in consumer hero panels and use the approved Korean editorial headline from the local guide when relevant; the corporate Korean signature remains separate.

No other translated VELA signature has been released. Local body copy can express the positioning without presenting a new line as a signature. A product page may omit an untranslated signature rather than invent one.

## Design language
Graphite backgrounds, Paper type, a restrained Signal detail and generous margins. Prefer close views of junctions, surfaces and useful components. A hero need not contain a person. Do not darken every product image so much that the specification becomes hard to inspect. The endorsed subbrand SVG retains masterbrand geometry and must not be redrawn in a thin luxury serif.

## Product care
For A8 range copy use the test conditions in Claims Review; the 110 km figure applies only to the EU/CH/UK configuration. A global English asset cannot silently include the US and Korean batteries in that result. Material statements about City C2 do not apply to VELA. The group owner count is not the VELA customer count.

## Working examples
“A little further from the rush.”
“Details worth coming back to.”
A web card might pair an engineering detail with a route observation. These phrases show tone, not a measurable performance or comfort statement. They are not required text. The proposed vibration comparison in the autumn media sheet remains subject to Claims Review; this studio guide releases no comparative claim.
