# Pack production / mechanical and copy standard


Packaging Standard PK-21 · version 2.1 · released 14 May 2026
Signatories: Owen Park, Jonas Reed, Ivo Sen. Controlled deliverable of board PS-0514, paired with Professional Systems handbook 2.1. The board owns professional carton legal-type specifications across both publications; changes require its signed production change record. Other packaging mechanics are owned by Packaging Engineering.

## Outer cartons
Identify the exact product, variant, market and required production markings. A lifestyle signature is optional and cannot replace model identification. Use the full endorsed logo where space permits, applying the identity manual's visible-size and clear-space measures. Consumer carton legal body type is at least 7 pt. Do not reduce a required qualification to fit a promotional statement: shorten or remove the statement instead.

## Germany / F4 service carton schedule
Minimum legal body type for the **German FIELD F4 service carton is 7 pt**, Graphite on Paper, as approved in PS-0514. Headings may be larger. This entry is specific to the German professional service carton. Changes require the release board's signed amendment; a studio may not replace this specification on the basis of a later export date.

## Hardware exception
For **DOCKGRID N6 connector-bay engraving only**, the DOCKGRID wordmark can omit SUPABRAND endorsement and use a minimum visible width of **18 mm**. Clearance to machined edges is 3 mm. This solves the small metal bay plate, not retail packaging or advertising. The full N6 outer carton uses the ordinary endorsed mark and the 36 mm minimum from the identity manual.

## Material and return panels
A recycled-frame claim is not a carton claim. Keep frame scope with any City C2 material copy. No universal recycled-content packaging statement has been approved. SECOND MILE may label eligible service instructions, but is not a certification badge.

For UK Q4 2026 battery take-back panels, the replacement legal sentence remains with UK counsel. The previous panel is not cleared for the new production batch. Layout may reserve space; this pack does not release wording for print. Do not borrow the German return paragraph or invent a UK sentence from general brand tone.

## Partner marks and print
No current alliance grant covers product packaging. A partner mark in a trade-show mockup is not a pack asset. All CMYK builds require a substrate proof. Safety and compliance symbols belong to controlled engineering artwork and are not included in this agency brand set. Blank placeholders in review layouts do not authorise release of a production carton.
