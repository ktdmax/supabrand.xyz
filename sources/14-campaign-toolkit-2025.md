# Less rush. More road. / launch pack


Studio launch pack · 17 March 2025 · issue 1.2
Approved: Group Brand and Europe Consumer Marketing. Flight 1 April–30 June 2025 inclusive.

## Idea
The city does not need another command to hurry. This season we show small choices that make an ordinary route feel personal: stopping for bread, taking the quieter street, arriving with time to talk.

**Less rush. More road.** replaces the masterbrand signature on this campaign's Austria, Germany and Netherlands consumer paid-display ads and Instagram placements. Scope: City C2 only. It does not replace the company signature in corporate pages, LinkedIn, packaging or retailer contracts. The campaign is now retained in the archive for measurement and reuse planning; artwork still requires a current campaign release before a new flight.

The campaign line remains English in the Netherlands. Austria: “Weniger Eile. Mehr Weg.” Germany: “Weniger Eile. Mehr Weg.” Supporting copy follows local address. No translated line outside these markets was part of the buy.

## Art direction
Paper field, a strong Signal rectangle, one useful product detail. The bicycle can sit off-centre; generous negative space holds the line. Cropped wheels are allowed in campaign photography if the scene remains intelligible; do not crop the identity marks. Example headline: “A detour can be part of the plan.” This was an alternate, not the released campaign signature.
