# Identity engineering / release drawings


Drawing set VI-24 · v2.4 · 02 Jun 2025 · approved by Ivo Sen, Head of Design Engineering
This release controls logo geometry, optical spacing and physical reproduction. Colour behaviour on screens is delegated to the Digital Design System. Campaign art direction cannot redraw marks.

## The open track
Our compact sign is two horizontal tracks joined by a rising diagonal. The interruption is intentional: movement needs an opening. The wordmark uses an original geometric alphabet supplied as filled paths, not a typed word in a commercial font. Never reconstruct it from office text. The primary horizontal lockup combines the sign with SUPABRAND. The secondary stacked lockup is for narrow covers. The compact sign is for avatars, app tiles and hardware where a nearby full name establishes ownership.

Use the dark mark on paper or light backgrounds; use the reversed mark on graphite. The signal-red variant is permitted on paper with no additional colour inside the mark. Keep all strokes, counters and proportions intact. Do not rotate, outline, add shadows or separate individual wordmark letters.

## Space and size
In supplied logo artwork, the wordmark cap height H is 70 drawing units. Clear space is at least H/2 on every side, measured from visible artwork, excluding file padding. Primary: minimum visible width 128 CSS px or 32 mm. Secondary: 96 CSS px or 24 mm. Compact sign: 20 CSS px or 5 mm. Pixel limits apply to rendered size, not the size of a downloaded file. The drawings have only transport padding; production must add the required clear space.

Endorsed subbrand signatures have SUPABRAND above the subbrand name; neither may be detached in consumer advertising. Minimum visible width: 144 CSS px / 36 mm, with clear space of half the endorsement's cap height. For an engraving on a Node N6 connector bay only, DOCKGRID may appear without endorsement at 18 mm, per Packaging § Hardware. This does not permit a compact retail-ad logo.

CMYK values are starting builds for coated stock, not mathematical equivalents to sRGB. Proof on the nominated substrate and obtain studio approval. Uncoated stock uses physical drawdowns; no approved spot-ink reference is supplied. Do not invent one. Signal is not the accessible small-button red; use the digital token for that role.

## Type and composition
Use the system sans stack in delivered browser templates. Editorial display trials use **Supa Grotesk Trial**, a fictional commissioned family. It is not included in this handover; its production licence has not been cleared in the studio ledger. Do not treat the name of a typeface as permission to distribute it. Office typography: Arial, then the installed system sans. Korean: installed Korean system sans. No downloadable font dependency is required for the supplied assets.

Work with 12 columns on wide pages, 6 on tablets, 4 on mobile; outer margins typically 6–8% of width. A strong left edge and generous blank space matter more than a fixed composition. Round functional cards by 12 px; full-bleed image fields can remain square. Grid and corner guidance may flex with content; logo geometry may not.

## Pictures, lines and movement
Show plausible daily use, serviced products and complete rider equipment appropriate to the scene. Avoid generic futuristic cities and speed blur that hides product detail. Illustration uses open contours, flat neutral fields and a single Signal accent. Icons use a 24-unit grid, 1.8-unit strokes, rounded joins and no filled circular badge by default. Icon labels are required when function would otherwise be ambiguous.

All source drawings in the asset folder are synthetic. Lifestyle placeholder plates are briefs, not documentary photographs and not evidence of product performance. The current photographer roster is maintained by Production and is not included here.
