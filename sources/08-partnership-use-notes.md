# Alliance desk / use of names and marks


Alliance desk circular, 03/07/2025 · edition 3.0
Sign-off: Elin Radu, Partnerships; Jonas Reed, Rights Counsel. Applies to use of partner names, logos, quoted relationship statements and jointly produced artwork. Commercial agreements do not automatically convey advertising rights.

## ORBITAL MESH — systems collaboration
Approved English wording: **“SUPABRAND works with ORBITAL MESH on connected fleet operations.”** Keep “works with” and “connected fleet operations”; do not substitute “powered by”, “certified by” or “exclusive partner”. The approved name is ORBITAL MESH, with a space. No alternate-language sentence has been signed off; in other-language materials retain the English relationship sentence or request translation approval.

Territory: all twelve released SUPABRAND markets and global corporate distribution. Grant: 2024-04-01 through 2026-12-31 inclusive. Review scheduled 2026-10-15 does not itself end the grant.
Channels: corporate website, corporate LinkedIn, press releases, corporate presentations; FIELD and DOCKGRID B2B product case studies on product websites, YouTube and trade-show panels. No consumer product advertising, ecommerce, retail packaging, Instagram or TikTok grant. Product communication is therefore permitted only for the named professional case-study channels. Corporate use is permitted only on the channels listed.

SUPABRAND leads the lockup at 1.4 times the partner wordmark's cap height. Separate marks by at least one SUPABRAND cap height and a thin vertical rule. The relationship sentence must accompany the lockup. Do not use the partner mark alone as a product feature. The supplied SVG is a corporate English example and contains the approved sentence.

The separate rights schedule for the co-produced **OM commuter portrait** is held by Production. Its post-2026 extension end date is not recorded in this circular. Do not infer image rights from the name-and-mark grant.

## VIAFORMA — the neighbourhood workshop programme
Approved wording: **“In collaboration with VIAFORMA.”**
Territories: Austria, Germany, France, Italy, Netherlands only.
Rights window: 2022-09-01 to **2025-06-30** inclusive. The commercial teams have not signed a renewal. From 2025-07-01 remove the relationship from live placements; dated historical reports may describe what happened without presenting a current affiliation. New production using the logo requires a new signed rights grant.

Within the original window: corporate event webpages, Instagram organic workshop announcements, print event brochures and workshop trade-show panels. No paid advertising, product pages, ecommerce, packaging or product endorsement. Corporate event communication was permitted; generic group endorsement was not.

Equal cap height, 1:1, with a one-cap-height gap. Approved sentence immediately below. Event photographers and venue signage had separate permissions. Existing vector artwork does not extend the contract.
