# How SUPABRAND sounds


Writing studio · October 7, 2025 · 2.3 · approved by Group Brand, with regional language leads

## Calm confidence
Write like someone who knows the product and has time for the person asking. Short sentences help; clipped slogans everywhere do not. Name a useful detail and explain what it changes. Avoid turning ordinary ownership into a test of courage or identity.

Before: “Revolutionise your urban lifestyle with unparalleled next-generation micromobility.”
After, as an example: “A place for your bag. A little room for the rest of your day.”
The second sentence is an illustration of tone, not a factual feature claim for every model. Check the actual carrier before adapting it.

## Three registers
Consumer: warm, direct, observant. Professional: practical, respectful of operators' expertise, specific about supported configurations. Corporate: accountable and restrained; distinguish a target from a result. Personality is qualitative guidance. A sentence can follow every formatting instruction and still need an editor's judgement.

## Terminology desk
| Prefer | Avoid / condition |
|---|---|
| electric-assist bicycle, e-bike | “motorbike” for City or Allroad |
| electric scooter | “e-bike” for PICO Glide |
| battery charging station | “universal charger” for DOCKGRID Node |
| entry-level specification | “cheap range” |
| maintenance and repair | “maintenance-free”, unless specifically evidenced |
| recycled aluminium in the frame | “recycled bike” |
| target, aim, intend | achieved, delivered when discussing an ambition |
| people who ride; riders | assuming all customers are athletes |

“Zero impact”, “planet-positive”, “carbon-neutral product”, “safest” and “number one” are not approved portfolio expressions. “Hidden champion” belongs in internal positioning discussion and cannot be converted into a leadership claim. Never create a certification seal from descriptive copy.

## Local voice
Use du in Austrian consumer copy; Sie in German consumer and service copy. Swiss German customer copy uses Sie and ss, never ß. Professional German-language tenders use Sie in all three markets. French uses vous without excessive formality. Italian consumer copy uses tu, while procurement letters may use an impersonal professional register. Korean uses respectful 해요체 for consumer editorial and 합니다체 for specifications and institutional copy. Local guides provide examples and narrow exceptions; they do not approve new engineering claims.

## A softer kind of ambition
“We make things for the way life actually moves.”
“Good design leaves you a little more room.”
“Für Wege, die zu Ihrem Leben passen.”
These are writing-room examples. The last one uses formal address and needs adaptation for an Austrian consumer placement. None is a replacement for the signature table.
