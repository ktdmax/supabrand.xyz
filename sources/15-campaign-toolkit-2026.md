# A little closer / autumn city campaign


Campaign brief AC-26 · v1.0 · approved 30 July 2026 by Group Brand and Consumer Marketing
Media window: **15 August–31 October 2026**, inclusive, according to placement-market local dates.

## The thought
A good city is made of reachable things: someone you know, somewhere to pause, a useful errand that becomes a ride. We make the product present without making it the whole story.

Released line: **Your city. A little closer.**
Austria/Germany: **Deine Stadt. Ein Stück näher.**
Netherlands: use the English line.

## Bought placements
This signature is required in place of the masterbrand tagline on campaign-tagged **paid digital advertising and Instagram** assets for **City C1 DAILY and City C2**, targeted to **Austria, Germany and Netherlands** during the media window. Instagram includes the campaign's organic launch tiles and paid amplification. Do not use this requirement for all brand posts or all consumer markets. Digital Action Red and legal legibility requirements survive the campaign art direction.

LinkedIn, corporate website, product detail pages, YouTube, TikTok, packaging, press releases and presentations are outside this signature substitution. They may discuss the campaign in dated editorial text, but use their ordinary signature treatment. VELA, PICO, FIELD and DOCKGRID are outside the campaign product scope.

## Language agreement
Germany's exact **Deine Stadt. Ein Stück näher.** headline is a signed creative exception to formal address for this line alone, approved jointly by Group Brand and Germany Language Lead for AC-26. All surrounding German copy and CTAs still use Sie/Ihr. Austria uses du throughout consumer copy. This paragraph does not authorise other informal German headlines.

## Execution notes
The hero can use an illustrated city contour and a parked bicycle. No landmark is compulsory. Headline placement in the upper left is a studio preference, not a pass/fail criterion. “Explore City C2” is sample English CTA copy, not a translation instruction.

## Release checklist used by media trafficking
The traffic sheet carries campaign code, model, locale, territory, channel, flight dates and destination. Consumer Marketing signs the media plan. New comparative copy additionally requires Claims Review. Font availability in an agency mockup is not a licence record. If a local team proposes a campaign beyond these markets, obtain an amended release rather than extending the approved creative by analogy.
