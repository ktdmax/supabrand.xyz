# SECOND MILE / speak with care


Impact Office note 2.2 · 11 March 2026 · endorsed by Finance Controls and Claims Review
SECOND MILE is the group's repair, parts and material-learning initiative. It is neither a product subbrand nor an environmental certification. Its name may appear as a programme heading in corporate communication and eligible service pages. Do not place it in a seal that implies a product passed an external environmental audit.

## Make the boundary part of the sentence
Our work starts with the products already in people's lives. A repair can be a practical choice; it is not automatically proof of a quantified carbon saving. Explain the service available in the reader's market before telling a large environmental story.

Claims Review maintains the released language in the Claims & Evidence Register. Every quantified statement needs its period, entity and denominator. Translations must preserve those boundaries. The 2025 group revenue share from eligible products is a finance measure; it does not count products or say that a percentage of each bicycle is circular.

## The ambition
Our 2030 ambition is to cut Scope 1 and 2 operational emissions by 40% from the 2022 baseline. It is a target for SUPABRAND Mobility SE's controlled operations, excluding product use and supply-chain Scope 3. The 2025 measurement is a 12% reduction on that baseline. Neither figure supports “40% lower-emission bikes” or “we have reached our 2030 goal”. Keep “aim” and the year in target copy.

## Material statements
City C2's frame statement concerns aluminium mass in its bare frame, not the complete bicycle, battery or retail package. Do not round 62% to two thirds. A frame claim cannot be carried onto C1 DAILY because the silhouettes look similar. The current C1 supply file does not release a recycled-content claim.

## Jurisdiction desk
In French consumer communication, every approved frame recycled-content claim must be accompanied in the same field of view by: **« Teneur mesurée sur le cadre en aluminium, hors batterie et composants. »** This is the France counsel-approved copy condition for our release, not a universal statement about legislation. Other markets retain the English claim's substantive scope in their local text but are not required to reproduce this French sentence.

The US City C2 warranty claim has its own territory and qualification; it must not be translated into European ecommerce. Country-specific statutory-rights copy is supplied by Legal, not created from this guide. The UK battery take-back panel for the Q4 2026 carton is awaiting the final counsel text; the current packet does not contain a released replacement.

## Recognition
If referring to the 2024 fictional Circular Practice Review recognition after its display licence ends, a dated corporate historical account may state the past result in words. A new promotional badge, current rating statement or 2026 release headline must not suggest continuing recognition. Claims Review has restricted the promotional wording to its recorded display period.

Do not use green scenery or a leaf icon to carry a stronger environmental proposition than the copy supports. SECOND MILE programme stories can be hopeful and specific without claiming universal sustainability.
