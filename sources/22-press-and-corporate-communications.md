## Boilerplate, released 12 August
SUPABRAND Mobility SE is a Vienna-headquartered mobility manufacturer building bicycles and selected mobility systems for people and professional operators. The SUPABRAND portfolio includes VELA, PICO, FIELD and DOCKGRID. Its City and Allroad bicycles are available in twelve markets. Learn more at supabrand.xyz.

Use the company name at first formal mention; SUPABRAND is the approved short name thereafter. Do not describe the organisation as Austrian-made across its range. Do not turn our internal hidden-champion ambition into an external ranking or claim market leadership without released evidence.

## Announcements
The current corporate signature follows the January brand letter. Product launches use the exact Product Operations name and release markets. For a press release with a claim, the Claims desk's wording and associated dossier govern the factual sentence. This deskbook approves the boilerplate and release workflow, not new technical or financial facts.

Release owner: Corporate Affairs. Financial results also need Finance Controls. New engineering comparisons need Claims Review and Vehicle Validation. Existing released facts still need their qualifications in the final piece. An executive quote is subject to the same limits as body copy.

## Recognition and past events
A dated historical paragraph can describe a past award with its year and corporate scope, per Impact Office restrictions. Do not place the expired 2024 recognition badge on a new 2026 press release or headline it as a current rating. Old logos in event photographs need image rights and contextual review; they do not establish a current partnership.

## Production requests
Our press intake form asks for headline, audience, market, locale, channel, publication date, named entity and attachments. It is an intake record, not a release. A request marked “complete” only means the form was filled out. Partner material requires its own rights evidence.
