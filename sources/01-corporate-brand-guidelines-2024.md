# SUPABRAND / The way we move


Brand office, Vienna · issue 3.2 · 12 February 2024
Approved: Mara Leitner, Group Brand Director. Distribution: agencies, commercial leads, product studios.

## A company built around the next kilometre
SUPABRAND Mobility SE is a privately held mobility manufacturer headquartered in Vienna. Founded in 2016 by a frame engineer, a service designer and a fleet mechanic, we build bicycles first. Components and final assembly are commissioned across a qualified manufacturing network; “Austrian-made” is not a company-wide origin statement. Our registered company name appears in contracts, corporate footers and formal first mentions. SUPABRAND is both the masterbrand and the approved short name. The company website is supabrand.xyz.

The company sells consumer bicycles and specialist mobility systems to employers, operators and workshops. We want to be the manufacturer that a mechanic recommends before an advertising campaign reaches you. Hidden champion is an internal description of that ambition, never evidence of a market ranking.

## Position
For people and organisations who want movement to fit everyday life, SUPABRAND makes thoughtful mobility products that are straightforward to use and worth looking after. We sit between disposable novelty and intimidating technical prestige. Engineering earns the trust; the experience makes it human.

Our master signature is **Move beyond.** Keep the full stop. Use it on corporate homepages, institutional end cards and brand-led brochure covers. Product specification sheets may omit it. The brand is not a promise that every trip, every product or every market is emission-free.

## The portfolio
SUPABRAND owns four endorsed subbrands. VELA is the premium bicycle expression, PICO the accessible electric-scooter business, FIELD the professional cargo-cycle business and DOCKGRID the business charging-station platform. These are brands, not separate legal entities. DOCKGRID stations support nominated SUPABRAND fleet batteries; they are not universal public charging infrastructure.

Five product families organise engineering and service: City, Allroad, Cargo, Glide and Node. City bicycles carry the masterbrand directly; the DAILY line is their entry-level specification, not a fifth subbrand. Allroad belongs to VELA, Cargo to FIELD, Glide to PICO and Node to DOCKGRID. A component shared between families does not transfer a claim or a warranty.

## An organisation, not a mood board
The Brand Director approves corporate identity. Product Operations owns commercial names; Design Engineering maintains artwork geometry. Market teams can commission translations within an approved localisation remit. They cannot change product eligibility, evidence or partner permissions. A photograph of a scooter in a market is not a sales announcement.

## Character in practice
Precise, open, quietly inventive. Explain the useful consequence of engineering before listing its complexity. Treat maintenance as part of ownership, not as an embarrassing afterthought. Avoid heroic speed narratives, superiority without a basis and pictures of unsafe riding. The brand's warmth should come from noticing ordinary life.

Present products at human scale: a folded map, a jacket on a carrier, a route that changes because a friend calls. Nothing requires a particular prop. Examples in this book show the desired attention, not a mandatory script.
