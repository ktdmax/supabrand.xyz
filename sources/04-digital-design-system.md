# Web foundations / implementation notes


DX foundations 1.8 · 2026-02-05 · approval: Nia Holt, Digital Design Lead
Applies to corporate website, product website, ecommerce and paid HTML display. This is the designated source for digital colour roles; it does not change the print palette or partner entitlements.

## Tokens that have a job
Primary actionable buttons use **Action Red #B72F28**, with white #FFFFFF text. Hover #942720; focus ring Graphite with a 3 px Paper separation. Signal #F04438 remains available for large decorative blocks and brand art, but must not substitute for Action Red on a primary web CTA. Disabled controls use Mist with Graphite text and an explicit disabled state; colour alone is insufficient. Links are Graphite, underlined in body copy. Do not style a decorative pill as a button.

Recommended body size is 16 CSS px and line height 1.5; legal qualifications accompanying a web claim must be at least 12 CSS px and visible in the same viewport section before purchase. A tooltip or an asterisk with no displayed text does not satisfy this placement. Supplied components are starting points: test keyboard focus, labels, contrast and zoom in their actual context.

## Components
A product card has a family eyebrow, exact released product name, short benefit, availability cue and a descriptive action. “Find your everyday” and “Explore the range” are sample CTAs. They are not mandatory vocabulary. Transactional CTAs should describe the next action: “Choose size”, “Request a fleet quote”, or locally approved equivalents.

At 60 rem and above, a 12-column page grid is a useful default. Mobile cards stack; never reduce body text to preserve a desktop screenshot. Keep technical specifications in labelled rows, separate from soft campaign language. A specification row must not inherit a claim from another card through visual proximity.

## Channels and destinations
Corporate pages describe the organisation; subbrand product pages carry the relevant endorsed logo. Ecommerce must show market availability before checkout. A global English page does not establish that every product is for sale in every English-speaking market. DOCKGRID and FIELD use enquiry journeys, not a consumer “Buy now” action.

Paid digital inherits these colour and legibility roles. Campaign art may replace a signature or background, not a destination's market gating. Partner badges require a cleared placement even when there is an empty badge slot in the template.
