# SUPABRAND
### Make room for moving.

Welcome to SUPABRAND Mobility SE, a fictional Vienna-headquartered mobility manufacturer with bicycles at its core and a growing professional systems business. Our brand address is **supabrand.xyz**. It is an identifier within this fictional project; no domain ownership, live website or real company affiliation is asserted.

**Start with [brandbook.html](brandbook.html)** — a responsive visual introduction that opens directly in a browser. It brings together the philosophy, original identity, product architecture and agency library. No installation, external fonts or account is needed.

## A little room for real life
Our purpose is to give everyday movement more possibility, with less friction. **Spielraum** is the creative idea behind it: room to choose a route, notice a detail, or make something work on an ordinary Tuesday. Our SUPA working principles are **Sinn, Umsicht, Präsenz, Alltag**. They were adopted after the name was coined, as a practical expression of our culture.

The masterbrand carries City bicycles, including the entry-level DAILY line. Four endorsed subbrands serve distinct needs: **VELA** premium Allroad bicycles, **PICO** Glide electric scooters, **FIELD** professional Cargo cycles and **DOCKGRID** Node fleet-battery charging stations. **SECOND MILE** is the group's repair, parts and material-learning programme.

## Working with the handover
This is an accumulated agency and Brand Operations library spanning 2022–2026, with a handover snapshot of **7 September 2026**. The source documents retain their original voices, approvals and periods of use. New agency work should be grounded in the relevant product, market, language, channel and production records.

- `sources/`: 25 source documents, including a Claims desk CSV and an evidence dossier with reproduced fictional extracts.
- `assets/`: original SVG identity artwork, endorsed logos, partner artwork, icons, colour files, concept illustrations, photography briefs and editable HTML/SVG examples.
- `source-register.csv`: inventory of documents, owners, versions and issue dates.
- `asset-register.csv`: inventory of artwork and its production/rights state.
- `corpus-index.md`: browsable library list.

The presentation is a visual introduction, not a replacement for specialist approvals in the sources. Samples show possible executions. A component, example phrase or retained artwork file is not by itself a licence to publish a new placement. Where a production record has not been supplied, contact the responsible team rather than filling the gap from a mockup.

Twelve example markets are covered, with thirteen working locales. English, German, French, Italian and Korean editorial material is included. Portfolio presence does not mean every model is sold in every market.
